# CRM Tasks change contract — agreed 16 November 2026

Scope: Tasks and task counters/navigation in Control Center only. Do not change other business sections.
Preserve existing task IDs, text, reports, original evidence and status. Never delete, recreate, auto-close or relabel historical completion as 1C verification during UI migration.
History contains ALL scoped task states across all dates, including missing/archived client cards. Pending, superseded and cancelled records remain discoverable.
Control Center overdue counter and its destination MUST use the same scoped records and overdue predicate. Carry-over cannot disappear on Monday. Do not deduplicate separate obligations only for display.
Approved weekly denominators stay traceable; cancelled tasks are not successful. Historical recorded completion is labelled separately from verified 1C shipments. Shelf checks never complete a shipment.
Create flow: manager → approved route day → prepare whole route → compact editable review → explicit confirmation. Exclude networks, preserve linked identities, no writes from preparation.
Before approval allow SKU search, add, remove, replace, quantities, title and result. Revalidate changed plans server-side against current stock. Keep other client drafts intact.
November–March: prioritize the actual climate/heating assortment when compatible with client evidence. Category metadata is imperfect: inspect canonical product/subgroup. Summer garden/washers require an explicit recorded reason; history alone is insufficient. Do not force heating on an unrelated client.
Test historical visibility, missing client, pagination, role scope, counter/list parity, 25/50 percentage, partial versus complete, SKU changes and seasonal exclusions. Run database fixture checks with rollback; inspect deployed UI. Do not claim hardware/voice tests not performed.

Season/assortment amendment: preserve repeat purchases and client revenue ABC alongside seasonal expansion. Read full available client history, explicitly compare previous Oct/Nov/Dec. Winter opportunities include heating/climate, snow blowers, chainsaws, log splitters, circulation pumps, humidifiers and allowed welding equipment; do not restrict to climate or label year-round tools winter-only. Inverter welding machines are forbidden for ALL field-manager clients, enforced in catalog, proposals, validation and creation RPC. Other welding types remain allowed. Voice route requests must choose the explicitly requested manager/date, not the selected historical task.

Route amendment 16 November 2026: user explicitly expands scope to director routes and their task visibility. Save an entire day transactionally via crm_route_day_v1; no per-row client-side mutation sequence. Separate opening another date from moving a saved day. Preserve IDs and visited history, reject occupied target, check loaded revision, retry with same request ID. Region recommendations stay in the chosen city or cities already in the route and never silently add all-manager geography. Saved route clients are visible in Tasks but are not sales tasks or KPI denominator until approved. Preserve previously approved task deadlines/weekly baselines when moving visits.

AIR3 amendment 16 November 2026: user authorizes glasses login and Lera voice reliability fixes. Preserve session security and other CRM business rules. Bound voice auth/network waiting, abort on stop/timeout, and never automatically replay paid audio or delayed voice commands. Hardware success requires observed playback and a real microphone test.

Task cost/support amendment 16 November 2026: keep GPT-5.4 mini and the agreed assortment/task rules. Direction selection must not resend the full monthly ledger; final SKU planning retains every available SKU/month, ABC, returns, prior autumn and client constraints. Do not automatically make a third paid repair call after validation fails. Keep the paid draft editable and unsigned, and validate current stock/policy for free before explicit approval. Large-client context must be encoded without arbitrary truncation. Reuse old paid cache keys when changing serializers; shared route preparation is permitted only after current caller access/role/route/facts checks. Personal report/transcript caches remain user-scoped. Opening/retrying unchanged facts must not charge again. Show measured input/output/cached tokens and cost, distinguish actual billing from estimates. Support fixes preserve ticket history and never close an unverified report.

Missing-task continuation: choose manager then a route day or the whole week. Authoritative coverage precedes any paid generation. Skip existing route assignments and the existing one-shipment-contract-per-legal-entity/week rule; do not merge client cards, trading points, routes or separate visits. Preserve paid drafts across refresh and changed stock/history. Exactly one preparation lease may own a working legal entity/week; other visits link to its original draft rather than generate another. Restoring and free current-stock validation must never call the model. A paid regeneration requires an explicit changed instruction, current draft revision and idempotent request ID. Cache hits never bypass current access, route or policy checks. Scope changes require visible review and do not alter approved task deadlines or weekly baselines.

Further cost audit: concurrent identical external voice preparations share one in-flight operation; changed commands, task/client/route or actors do not. Re-saving an unchanged report must not buy the same automatic assessment again; changed verification, shelf, profile or cutoff facts invalidate its saved assessment. Private read-only answers and shelf parsing reuse exact previously paid results only after fresh access/fact checks; do not share personal results across actors. New working-plan response schemas group equal per-SKU quantity maxima without changing accepted SKU/quantity pairs. Derive working shipment stage and the canonical 1C result on the server rather than paying to generate fields that are overwritten. Keep full purchase history and both assortment-selection stages; do not trade quality for a speculative call reduction. Measure per-stage provider tokens, cached/reasoning tokens and technical payload sizes; never cap accounting at the reserved amount or label estimates as actual monthly billing.
